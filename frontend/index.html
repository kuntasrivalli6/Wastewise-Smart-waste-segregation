Why Household Segregation Matters

Proper segregation at source transforms garbage from a toxic hazard into valuable resources.

⚠️

The Mixed Garbage Crisis

When wet kitchen scraps are mixed with plastics, glass, or batteries, the entire load becomes contaminated and ends up in massive smoking landfills like Ghazipur or Deonar.

🌱

Organic Biomethanation

Pure segregated wet waste decomposes cleanly into high-grade organic compost or biomethane gas that powers community kitchens and vehicles across Indian cities.

🤝

Worker Safety & Dignity

Separating sanitary and hazardous waste in clearly marked wrappers shields thousands of formal and informal sanitation workers from infections and lacerations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common segregation inquiries answered by environmental guidelines.

Why can't I recycle greasy pizza boxes?

Food oil and melted cheese soak into paper fibers, preventing pulp mills from separating paper fibers during recycling. Tear off the clean lid for recycling, and put the oily base into residual waste.

How should I dispose of used sanitary napkins safely?

Wrap the soiled pad in 3-4 layers of newspaper and mark it clearly with a visible Red Cross or Red Dot. Hand it over separately to your waste collector so sanitation workers can handle it without opening or injury.

Can I throw spent AA batteries in my general trash?

Never. Batteries contain toxic heavy metals that leach into groundwater. Tape the terminal ends with tape and deposit them at dedicated municipal hazardous waste or e-waste collection kiosks.
