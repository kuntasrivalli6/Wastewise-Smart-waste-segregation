Civic Agreement

Terms of Service

1. Platform Purpose & Scope

Waste Wise is a civic educational platform intended to promote responsible source segregation in alignment with the Solid Waste Management Rules (2016) promulgated by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India.

2. Municipal Supremacy

While our rule-based classification engine is backed by national solid waste management standards, specific municipal corporations, councils, and Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) may enact localized segregation or collection schedules. Users are encouraged to verify distinct local collection practices.

3. Points & Rewards Policy

Waste Wise points, achievement tiers (Green Starter, Eco Warrior, Earth Guardian, Sustainability Champion), and civic badges represent digital milestones recognizing proactive environmental behavior. Points possess no direct monetary cash value, cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, and cannot be transferred between accounts.

4. Prohibited Conduct

Users agree not to tamper with the reward ledger, automate synthetic classification queries, or attempt unauthorized privilege escalation to administrator roles. Any tampering will result in immediate session revocation.
